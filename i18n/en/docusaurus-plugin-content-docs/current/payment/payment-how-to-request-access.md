## Requesting access to make Payments

If you are a Woovi customer and want to request the payments feature, it is quick and simple:
To request the payments feature, just contact Woovi support through the chat available on the platform. In the chat, an agent will help you and provide all the information needed to enable the feature.

Keep in mind that to request the payments feature you need an active Woovi account. If you don't have an account yet, create one for free and then contact support to enable the payments feature.
