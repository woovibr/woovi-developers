## Adding funds to make Payments

Adding funds on Woovi is quick and simple.

- Open `Contas` (Accounts) in the side menu
- Click `Detalhes da Conta` (Account details) on the account you want to add funds to
- In the `Detalhe da conta` (Account details) section, find your account's Pix key
- In your bank's app, transfer the desired amount to your Woovi account's Pix key

![account pix key](/img/payment/add-funds-payment.png)

Now you can use the funds to make Payments on Woovi!
