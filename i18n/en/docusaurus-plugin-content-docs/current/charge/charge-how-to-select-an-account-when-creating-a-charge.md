title: How to select an account when creating a charge
tags:
  - qrcode
  - charge
  - payment
---

1 - In the charge creation form, go to the Destino do Pagamento (Payment Destination) step

![charge-creation](/img/charge/charge-creation.png)

2 - Open the collapsible, click the account field, and the account search drawer will open; select or search for the account you want to use for the charge

![charge-account-drawer](/img/charge/charge-account-drawer.png)

:::info
- Account selection only appears for customers who have more than one virtual account.
- When this charge is paid, its amount will appear in the statement of the selected account.
:::
