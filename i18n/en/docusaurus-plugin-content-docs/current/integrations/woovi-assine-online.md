## What is Assine Online?

Assine Online is a platform that provides a digital signature solution for businesses.
Users can sign documents using their mobile phone or computer.

## How to integrate Assine Online with Woovi?

To integrate Assine Online with Woovi, you must:

1. Create an account on Assine Online
2. Create an application on Assine Online [here](https://developers.assine.online/docs/starting/#criando-um-aplicativo)
3. Contact Woovi support to set up the integration
