A Static QRCode is a QRCode that accepts multiple payments.

It lets you set a predetermined amount or let the payer choose the amount at payment time

## Use Cases

- Accept payments at in-person events
- Accept payments in [Twitch](https://www.twitch.tv/) live streams
- Accept donations
- Accept payments in physical stores
- Accept payments in `vending machines`
- Accept payments at [https://www.etiquetas.io/](https://www.etiquetas.io/)
