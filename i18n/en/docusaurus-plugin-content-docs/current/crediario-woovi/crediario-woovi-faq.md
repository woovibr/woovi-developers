## FAQ – Buy Now Pay Later Woovi

## Can I create a Buy Now Pay Later Woovi plan for CPF or CNPJ?
Yes, you can create it for individuals or legal entities

## When does Buy Now Pay Later Woovi come into effect?
Only after payment of the first installment.

## What happens if the customer does not pay the first installment?
The Buy Now Pay Later Woovi plan is marked as expired and all its installments are expired as well.
