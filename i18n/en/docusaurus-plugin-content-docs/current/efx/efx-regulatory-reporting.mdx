Woovi reports **every eFX operation** to the Central Bank of Brazil, in the **ACAM225** document. You do not need to send any file or call any extra route: Woovi files the report from the payment data.

## Your responsibility

The report uses the data you send when creating the payment. It must be **accurate and truthful**:

| Data | Field |
| --- | --- |
| Purpose of the operation (goods or services) | `purpose` |
| Payer in Brazil | `payer.name`, `payer.taxID` |
| Beneficiary and the CNR | `beneficiary` |
| Operation invoice | `invoice.number`, `invoice.issuedAt`, `invoice.url` |
| Amount | `value` |

Pick `purpose` by what is actually being paid: `GOODS_PURCHASE` for the purchase of goods and `SERVICE_PURCHASE` for the purchase of services.

:::warning
Do not reuse invoices or send generic data. Each payment must match a real operation between the payer and your platform, with its own invoice.
:::
