## Before you integrate

| Requirement | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Non-resident BRL account (CNR) | Opened at a Brazilian institution authorized to operate in the foreign exchange market (BCB Resolution No. 277, art. 67). Woovi settles payments into it. |
| Holder allowed to move third-party funds | If the CNR moves third-party funds, the holder must be a financial institution supervised in its home country (BCB Resolution No. 277, art. 68). |
| Brazilian CNPJ | The foreign entity needs a CNPJ registration. |
| KYC/KYB with Woovi | Company documents with apostille and sworn translation, identification of the ultimate beneficial owners (UBO) and the license for the activity. |
| Pix key on the CNR | The CNR must have a registered Pix key. |
| CNR registered at Woovi | The CNR sent in `beneficiary.bankAccount` must be registered at Woovi for your company. Otherwise, creation answers `400 beneficiary.bankAccount is not a registered CNR account`. |
| eFX API enabled | Woovi enables eFX for your company at the end of onboarding. Without it, the routes answer `403 eFX is not enabled for this company`. |
| Application with scopes | The application (AppID) must use scopes and have `EFX_PAYMENT_CREATE` (quote and create) and `EFX_PAYMENT_LIST` (get and list). An application without scopes gets `403 application must use scopes` on every eFX route. |

Calls use the same `Authorization: <AppID>` header as the rest of the Woovi API. If the application has allowed IPs, they apply here too.

## Rules for every operation (BCB Resolution No. 561/2026)

:::warning
These rules apply to **every** operation. A payment that breaks them is not settled into your CNR.
:::

- **The payer is your customer.** Each payment belongs to a customer of your platform, identified in `payer`.
- **The Pix comes from an account in the payer's own name.** The CPF or CNPJ of the paying account must be the same as `payer.taxID`. A Pix paid by someone else is not settled and goes to refund (see [Refunds](./efx-refunds.mdx)).
- **No netting.** Pay-ins and pay-outs are not netted: each payment is settled for its full amount, without offsetting other amounts.
- **US$ 10,000 limit per operation.** Each payment's `value`, converted by the Central Bank's PTAX selling rate (no margin), cannot exceed US$ 10,000. Above that, creation answers `400`.
- **No stablecoins in settlement.** Settlement is in reais, into your CNR. Stablecoins cannot be used to settle the operations.

## Data you send

Each payment carries the purpose (`purpose`), the payer (`payer`), the beneficiary (`beneficiary`) and the invoice (`invoice`). This data goes into the operation's report to the Central Bank, so it must be accurate. See [Central Bank reporting](./efx-regulatory-reporting.mdx).
