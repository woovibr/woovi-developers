### What is a charge?

A charge is a payment request via Pix. You can configure a new charge with `Customer`, `Value`, `Comment` and other identifying information.

### How do I create a charge?

To create a new charge, open the `Cobranças` (Charges) menu

![Menu Charge](/img/FAQ/charge-menu.png)

Click `Nova cobrança` (New charge)

![New Charge](/img/FAQ/new-charge.png)

You can configure the charge however you need.

![Charge](/img/FAQ/charge.png)

### How do I set a charge's expiration time?

Charge expiration can be configured in the `Ajustes` (Settings) menu

![Menu Settings](/img/FAQ/settings-menu.png)

In the `Pix` tab

![Settings Pix](/img/FAQ/settings-pix.png)

Find the `Tempo de Expiração` (Expiration Time) card and configure it as you wish.

![Expiration Time](/img/FAQ/expiration-time.png)

### How do I pay a charge?

You can pay a charge on our platform in several ways, such as:

- Copying the charge's QR Code and paying via Pix copy and paste

![QR Code Copy Paste](/img/FAQ/charge-copy-paste.png)

Example of a QR Code:

- Sending the payment link to your customer, as shown in the previous topic

### What is a payment link and how does it work?

As the name says, the payment link is a payment address you can use to send a charge to your customers so they can pay it.

![Payment Lik](/img/FAQ/payment-link.png)

- To pay, you can scan the QR code using your preferred payment app.
- Or copy the QR Code and paste it into your preferred payment app.
