### Why can't I open an account?

We do not accept companies or institutions involved in the following activities:

- Raffles, betting and gambling;
- Illegal drugs;
- Firearms;
- Counterfeit products;
- Pornography and adult content;
- Copyright and/or intellectual property infringement.

If your account opening request was rejected and you believe your company complies with our policies, contact support.
