The platform lets you register your customers.

A customer represents someone who pays for some of your products or services.

Registering customers lets you find out who your best customers are.

You can also send notifications and marketing campaigns to sell more to your existing customers.

## Use Cases

- Learn your customers' profile
- Engage existing customers with promotions
- Find out who your best customers are
