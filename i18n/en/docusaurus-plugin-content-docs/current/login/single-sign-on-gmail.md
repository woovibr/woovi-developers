## How It Works

Domain restriction is a security feature that lets you define which email domains can create accounts and access your organization on Woovi.

### Benefits

- **Improved security**: Only users from authorized domains can access
- **Access control**: Manage who can sign up to your organization
- **Prevention of unauthorized access**: Blocks login attempts from domains that are not allowed

### Configuration

To configure domain restrictions, open your organization settings and define the allowed domains. Only users with email addresses from these domains will be able to create accounts and access your organization.

### Usage Examples

- **Companies**: Restrict access to employees with corporate emails only
- **Educational organizations**: Allow only emails from specific educational institutions
- **Specific projects**: Limit access to domains of specific partners or customers

## Step-by-Step Configuration

### 1. Open the Organization Settings

1. Log in to your Woovi account
2. Go to **Configurações da Empresa** (Company Settings) > **Login por** (Login by)
3. Look for the **Restrições de domínio de email** (Email domain restrictions) section

### 2. Add Allowed Domains

1. Click **Restringir a domínios específicos** (Restrict to specific domains)
2. Type the domain you want to allow (e.g. `empresa.com`)
3. Press **Enter** or click **Adicionar** (Add) to add a new domain
4. Click **Salvar** (Save)

### 3. Settings explained

- **Restringir a domínios específicos** (Restrict to specific domains): Only emails from the configured domains can access
- **Permitir todos** (Allow all): Removes all restrictions (not recommended)

### Common Issues

**User can't access even with the correct domain:**

- Check that the domain matches the configuration exactly
- Make sure there are no extra spaces in the configuration
- Test with different subdomains if applicable

**Restrictions are not working:**

- Check that the feature is enabled
- Make sure the settings were saved correctly
- Test in incognito mode to avoid cache
