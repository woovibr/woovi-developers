*Using Single Sign-On is strongly recommended, as it provides a better user experience and increases security.*

## Setting up Single Sign-On (SSO)

Woovi supports multiple SSO identity providers (IdP). An Identity Provider is a server that can provide identity information about your company's users. For example, Google can be an identity provider for companies that use G Suite. If your company uses G Suite, you can sign in to Woovi automatically with your Google account; a Google server will then send your identity information to this site.

### Azure Single Sign On

If your company uses Azure Single Sign On, you can sign in to Woovi automatically using Azure as the IdP. To use this login strategy, you need to enable this option in the settings menu.

### Google Single Sign On

If your company uses G Suite, you can sign in to Woovi automatically using Google as the IdP.

### SAML Single Sign on

If your company uses SAML, you can sign in to Woovi automatically using your SAML servers as the IdP.
