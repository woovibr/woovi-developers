To apply discounts by the Pix payment method in your Woocommerce store, follow these steps:

## 1 - Install the WooCommerce Discounts Per Payment Method plugin

The first step is to install the [WooCommerce Discounts Per Payment Method](https://br.wordpress.org/plugins/woo-payment-discounts/) plugin in your Woocommerce store. This plugin adds the discount-per-payment-method option to your store.

<img width="974" alt="image" src="https://user-images.githubusercontent.com/67057187/226738836-31bf9083-dbcd-4110-a7be-55e44b997d30.png" />

## 2 - In the Woocommerce menu, select: Desconto por Método de Pagamento (Discount by Payment Method)

After installing the plugin, go to your store's admin panel and select the "Desconto por Método de Pagamento" (Discount by Payment Method) option. It should be available in the installed plugin's menu.

<img width="515" alt="Screen Shot 2023-03-21 at 17 56 58" src="https://user-images.githubusercontent.com/67057187/226739018-91cd8b10-7138-4a70-bc2f-99369946a137.png" />

## 3 - Configure the discount rules for the available payment methods

Finally, configure the discount rules for the payment methods available in your store. To apply a discount to Pix payments, select the "Pix" option and set the discount percentage you want to offer customers who choose this payment method.

![image](https://user-images.githubusercontent.com/67057187/226739290-cae6960f-b83f-40bd-a7cb-144ec8cef982.png)

<img width="1157" alt="image" src="https://user-images.githubusercontent.com/67057187/226739469-b91a2045-bcb5-4a6c-8524-c14c06adfc71.png" />

Remember to save the settings before leaving the page. With these simple steps, you can apply Pix payment method discounts in your Woocommerce store and offer your customers a differentiated experience.
