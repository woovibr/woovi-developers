## Basis Points

`Basis points`, `bips` or just `bps` is a unit of measure frequently used by financial institutions to represent percentages.

`1 bps` equals `0.01%`.

Basis points are mainly used to make communication clearer; saying that a percentage changed can be ambiguous, as in the following hypothetical situation:

`The credit interest rate is 10%`

Saying: `The rate will increase by 5%`

It is not clear whether the new rate will be 5% higher than it was, meaning the new rate is `10.5%`, or whether it increased by 5 percentage points and is now `15%`.

Now, saying `The interest rate increased by 50 basis points` brings clarity, and a reader could easily tell that the new rate is `10.5%`
