:::info
- First, make sure your company already has Loyalty Cashback enabled. If it isn't enabled, you can follow this [step by step](./cashback-fidelity-how-to-config.md) to enable it.
:::

To build customer loyalty on the platform, follow the steps below:

### 1. Open `Cashback` from the side menu

- ![](/img/cashback/cashback-sidemenu.png)

### 2. Click `New Cashback`

:::info
Keep in mind that the user must have admin permission on the platform
:::

- ![](/img/cashback-fidelity/cashback-overview-with-new-cashback-button.png)

### 3. Create the Loyalty Cashback

Fill in the form with the customer and the amount you want to give as Cashback

- ![](/img/cashback-fidelity/cashback-fidelity-create-screen.png)

Now the next charge created for this customer will include the Cashback you gave in this operation.
