To enable Loyalty Cashback in your company, follow the steps below:

## 1. Enable Loyalty Cashback

- Open `Cashback` from the side menu
- ![cashback fidelity sidemenu](/img/cashback/cashback-sidemenu.png)

On your first access, you will be taken to the Loyalty Cashback Tutorial.

![cashback fidelity tutorial](/img/cashback/cashback-tutorial.png)

> If you have already configured your cashback and want to review the settings, [learn more here](/docs/cashback-fidelity/cashback-fidelity-how-to-config)

### 1.1 Configure Loyalty Cashback

- Select enable, the percentage of cashback to be generated for the next purchase, and the minimum charge amount that generates cashback for the next purchase

| Field                    | Description                                                                           |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| enable/disable           | enables the feature                                                                   |
| percentage               | percentage to be generated for the next purchase, based on the paid charge amount     |
| minimum charge amount    | minimum charge amount required to generate cashback for the next purchase             |

When you enable the Loyalty Cashback feature, you will be redirected to the overview page.

![cashback fidelity overview](/img/cashback-fidelity/cashback-fidelity-overview.png)

## 2. Create a Charge with Loyalty Cashback

Once Loyalty Cashback is enabled, charges will include the option to enable it.

For the Loyalty Cashback option to be available, the charge must meet the minimum amount set in the configuration.

![cashback fidelity charge form add](/img/cashback-fidelity/cashback-fidelity-charge-create-add-collapsible.png)

After filling in the minimum charge amount, click to create the charge. You will be redirected to the charge detail:
![cashback fidelity charge detail](/img/cashback-fidelity/cashback-fidelity-charge-detail.png)
